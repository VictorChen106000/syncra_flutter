Recommended asset folders:

assets/
  logo/
    syncra_logo.png
  images/
    login_bg.jpg
    profile.jpg

Where to find assets:
- App/logo: export your existing Syncra logo from Figma as PNG, 1024x1024, transparent background.
- Login background: search on Unsplash or Pexels for abstract AI, dark gradient, futuristic workspace, or neural network background.
- Profile image: use your own placeholder image or a generated avatar. Keep it as profile.jpg.

After adding assets, register them in pubspec.yaml:

flutter:
  assets:
    - assets/images/
    - assets/logo/
